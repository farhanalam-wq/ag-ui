# ag-ui — Turn Any Website Into an Intelligent Assistant

## What is this?

ag-ui is a product that turns any company website into a knowledgeable, on-brand AI assistant you can talk to.

Give it a website address — for example, a startup, a store, a SaaS product, a university — and ag-ui reads and learns that site. A few moments later you can ask it anything about that company in plain language and get a clear, helpful answer, with links back to where the answer came from.

Think of it as: **paste a link, get a smart assistant for that business.**

## The problem it solves

Company websites are full of answers, but they are hard to explore. Information is scattered across pricing pages, product pages, docs, blogs, and about pages. Visitors end up clicking around, skimming, or giving up and contacting support.

ag-ui flips that. Instead of hunting through pages, you just ask:

- What does this company actually do?
- How much does it cost, and what's the difference between plans?
- How do I get started or integrate it?
- Where are they located, how do I contact them?
- What's new or noteworthy?

And you get a direct answer, grounded in what the site actually says — not a guess.

## How it feels to use it

**1. Share a website.**
You enter a company URL. ag-ui explores the public pages, figures out what's important, and organizes what it finds.

**2. It builds a living profile of the company.**
Not just a copy of the text, but an understanding: what the products are, what pricing looks like, key facts like contact details and locations, and how the brand looks and feels.

**3. You chat with it.**
Ask questions in a chat window, by voice, or through a small chat bubble embedded on any website. Answers arrive as natural conversation, supported by:

- **Trusted sources** — every answer shows which pages it came from, so you can verify it.
- **Beautiful visuals when helpful** — instead of a wall of text, you might see a pricing comparison, product highlights, key stats, a timeline, or a map, styled to match the company's own branding.
- **Memory of the conversation** — you can follow up, dig deeper, and explore without repeating yourself.

If the website changes, ag-ui can re-learn it and keeps a clean version history, so answers always reflect a specific, known snapshot of the site.

## What it is trying to become

ag-ui is aiming to be the easiest way for any business to offer an intelligent front door to everything they know.

The vision is:

- **For visitors:** no more digging through menus and docs. Just ask and understand, instantly.
- **For businesses:** a helper that never sleeps — one that answers customer questions, explains products, highlights pricing clearly, captures interest, and sounds like you, because it learned from your site and adopts your look and feel.
- **For the web at large:** any site can be conversational. A small snippet can add a floating assistant to an existing site — no rebuild required — turning a static website into an interactive guide.

In short, ag-ui wants to replace aimless browsing with conversation, and replace made-up chatbot answers with answers you can trust and check.

## Who is it for?

- **Customers and curious visitors** who want fast, honest answers about a company.
- **Founders and teams** who want their website to answer questions, explain offerings, and help sell, without adding more support burden.
- **Anyone managing knowledge** — marketing sites, documentation, catalogs, program info — who wants that knowledge to be askable, not just readable.

## What makes it different?

- **It learns from the real site**, not from generic knowledge. If it's not on the site, it doesn't pretend.
- **It shows its work.** Sources and citations are first-class, not an afterthought.
- **It looks like the brand it represents.** Colors, style, and tone adapt so the assistant feels like a natural part of the company, not a generic add-on.
- **It meets people where they are.** Full-screen workspace, simple chat, voice conversation, or a tiny embeddable bubble on any third-party site.

## What it is not

- It is not a generic chatbot that makes things up.
- It is not a search box that just dumps ten blue links on you.
- It is not a website builder or a analytics dashboard. It sits on top of what already exists and makes it conversational.

## Where it is headed

Today ag-ui can learn a site and hold a grounded, sourced conversation about it, with visuals and brand styling.

It is growing toward live voice conversations, deeper coverage of large sites, always-fresh knowledge that updates itself, and one-line embedding everywhere — so any business can say: here is our link, now our site can talk.
