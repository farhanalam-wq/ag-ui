# ag-ui — Turn Any Website Into an Intelligent Assistant

> **Paste a link, get a smart assistant for that business.**

ag-ui turns any company website into a knowledgeable, on-brand assistant you can talk to — by chat or by voice.

Give it a website address — a startup, a store, a SaaS product, a university — and ag-ui reads and learns that site. A few moments later you can ask anything about that company in plain language and get a clear, helpful answer, with links back to where the answer came from.

## The problem it solves

Company websites are full of answers, but they are hard to explore. Information is scattered across pricing pages, product pages, docs, blogs, and about pages. Visitors end up clicking around, skimming, or giving up and contacting support.

ag-ui flips that. Instead of hunting through pages, you just ask:

- What does this company actually do?
- How much does it cost, and what's the difference between plans?
- How do I get started or integrate it?
- Where are they located, how do I contact them?
- What's new or noteworthy?

You get a direct answer, grounded in what the site actually says — not a guess.

## How it works

**1. Share a website.**

Enter a company URL in the Ingestion Studio. ag-ui automatically discovers the public pages — checking sitemaps, site structure, and guides — and shows you a ranked list of what it found.

You stay in control: pick all pages, the top 20, or a custom set. Advanced controls let you tune how fast it reads.

**2. It learns the business.**

ag-ui reads the pages you selected and builds a living profile of the company:

- What the products and offerings are
- What pricing and plans look like
- Key facts — contact details, locations, links, social handles
- How the brand looks and feels — logo, colors, style

You watch it happen live with step-by-step progress: discovering, reading, organizing, ready. If a page fails to load, it is logged and skipped — it never blocks the whole learn.

**3. You talk to it — by chat or by voice.**

Once ready, the company appears in your workspace and you can start asking immediately.

## Chat workspace

The full-screen workspace is where you explore in depth:

- **Company switcher:** jump between every site you have taught it, each with its own colors and context.
- **Suggested starting points:** each company opens with tailored prompts like pricing tiers, products and APIs, headquarters and contact options — one click to ask.
- **Natural conversation:** ask in plain language, follow up, dig deeper. It remembers the thread so you never repeat yourself.
- **Clear answers:** formatted responses with headings, lists, and code blocks where helpful, plus copy in one click.
- **New chat anytime:** reset the thread without losing the learned knowledge.

## Answers you can trust

Every text answer shows its work:

- **Sources panel:** each answer lists the exact pages it used, with title, link, relevance match, and quoted snippet.
- **Inspect on demand:** open the Sources drawer to verify, copy a snippet, or jump to the original page.
- **Grounded in the site:** if it is not on the site, it does not pretend. Answers come from the learned snapshot, not generic knowledge.
- **Versioned knowledge:** each learn creates a clean snapshot. If the website changes, re-learn it — answers always reflect a specific, known version.

## Voice conversation

Talk instead of typing, in the workspace or in the embed:

- **One-tap mic button** next to the chat box with live audio levels.
- **Real conversation:** ask out loud, hear the spoken answer, interrupt and continue naturally.
- **Transcript in chat:** when the call ends, what you and the assistant said lands in the thread as readable text so you keep context.
- **Private by company:** switching companies mid-call never leaks one company's conversation into another.
- **Clear states:** connecting, live, mic blocked, or unavailable — you always know what is happening.

## Embed on any website

Turn any existing site into a conversational site with one snippet — no rebuild required:

- **Floating assistant bubble:** paste a single `<script>` tag with your assistant key. Visitors get a familiar orb in the corner that opens a chat panel.
- **Zero setup for visitors:** no logins, no IDs in the URL. It just opens and is ready to answer.
- **On-brand automatically:** the bubble and panel pick up the company's colors and name. Title and accent can be overridden per placement.
- **Honest status:** shows Ready when it can chat, Indexing while it is still learning, and Offline if disabled — never a dead button.
- **Voice included:** the embedded panel has the same mic and voice conversation as the workspace.
- **Safe to drop anywhere:** lightweight loader, lazy-loads the panel only when opened, isolated from the host page styles.

## Feels like the brand

ag-ui adopts the look of the company it represents — primary color, accents, and avatar — so the assistant feels like a natural part of the business, not a generic add-on. Switch from one company to another and the whole workspace re-themes instantly.

## Manage what it knows

- **Library of learned companies:** see everything indexed, with doc counts and readiness at a glance.
- **Re-learn anytime:** re-run discovery on a domain to refresh products, pricing, or new pages.
- **Pick what matters:** exclude thin or irrelevant pages during selection so answers stay sharp.
- **Start chatting instantly:** jump from the library straight into chat with any ready company.

## Who it is for

- **Customers and curious visitors** who want fast, honest answers about a company.
- **Founders and teams** who want their website to answer questions, explain offerings, and help sell — without adding support burden.
- **Anyone managing knowledge** — marketing sites, docs, catalogs, program info — who wants that knowledge to be askable, not just readable.

## What makes it different

- **It learns from the real site**, not from generic knowledge.
- **It shows its work.** Sources and citations are first-class, not an afterthought.
- **It talks and listens.** Full voice conversation alongside chat, everywhere it lives.
- **It looks like the brand it represents.** Colors and identity adapt automatically.
- **It meets people where they are.** Full-screen workspace for deep dives, tiny bubble for any third-party site.

## What it is not

- It is not a generic chatbot that makes things up.
- It is not a search box that dumps ten blue links on you.
- It is not a website builder or analytics dashboard. It sits on top of what already exists and makes it conversational.

## Where it is headed

Today ag-ui can learn a site and hold a grounded, sourced conversation about it by chat and voice, styled to the brand and embeddable anywhere.

It is growing toward deeper coverage of very large sites, always-fresh knowledge that updates itself, and one-line embedding everywhere — so any business can say: here is our link, now our site can talk.
